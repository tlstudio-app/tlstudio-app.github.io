Werewolf Game: Night Claw · Halloween 2026 The dead are back.

For Halloween, we brought the dead back into the game with a brand-new role, a transformed village and a darker atmosphere designed from the ground up.

Enter the night

A new role

The Necromancer

For the first time, being eliminated doesn’t mean you’re out of the game.

  1. Night A player is killed while the village sleeps.
  2. 20seconds Their ghost awakens and can speak only through gestures. No words, just hands, faces and whatever they can mime before the time runs out.
  3. Trust The Necromancer knows every ghost’s true identity, and decides whom to believe.

The whole game changes

Halloween takes over the village.

Cobwebs, bats, graves, fog and a darker atmosphere transform the village for Halloween. The same home screen players know by heart, redrawn for one night of the year.

How Night Claw is played

One phone. One table. No game master.

Werewolf Game: Night Claw runs the entire game from a single phone, so nobody has to sit out as the game master.

The phone travels around the table. Each player discovers their role in secret, then passes it on. A narrator voice, music and sound carry the night, and everyone else is free to lie, accuse and laugh across the table.

It is not a game you play on a screen. It is a game you play with each other.

Selfies, not avatars

Your friends become the characters.

When players join a game, they take a selfie. From then on, those faces are the village: the ones the werewolves choose between, the ones accused at dawn, the ones that come back as ghosts.

On Halloween night, that usually means fake blood, witch hats and a very unconvincing vampire.

After the last vote

Every game leaves a memory.

When the night ends, Night Claw puts together a recap of the game: who won, who lost, every player’s selfie next to the role they were secretly playing.

It is the moment everyone finds out who was lying all along. And it is something to keep, or send to the group chat once the table is cleared.
